SECTION 1: CORE GOVERNANCE & OPERATIONAL POLICIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE 1 [QDL-01]: Post-Quantum Cryptographic (PQC) Migration Mandate
Category: Compliance & Cryptography
Policy Statement:
  All internal communications, client key exchanges, and software build pipelines must enforce NIST-standardized Post-Quantum Cryptography (ML-KEM/Kyber for key encapsulation and ML-DSA/Dilithium for digital signatures). Legacy RSA-2048 and classical ECC-256 are banned from all production network interfaces.

RULE 2 [QDL-02]: Cryogenic Dilution Refrigerator Operation Protocols
Category: Operational Conduct & SOP
Policy Statement:
  Personnel entering the dilution refrigeration bay must hold Level 3 Quantum Cryogenic certification. Maintaining the 15 millikelvin superconducting qubit state requires continuous helium-3/helium-4 mixture ratio verification. Any anomalous temperature rise exceeding 40 mK mandates immediate quantum circuit job suspension.

RULE 3 [QDL-03]: Quantum Circuit Compiler & Pulse Optimization Protection
Category: Anti-Leak & Asset Protection
Policy Statement:
  Proprietary microwave pulse shaping algorithms, quantum error correction (QEC) topological surface codes, and circuit transpilation optimizers are classified as Tier-1 Core IP. Code repositories are watermarked, audited on commit, and inaccessible from non-whitelisted IP ranges.

RULE 4 [QDL-04]: Air-Gapping Academic & External Research Collaborators
Category: Data Security & Access
Policy Statement:
  External university researchers and visiting fellows submitting quantum algorithms to the cloud quantum simulator must execute jobs through sanitized QASM/QIR intermediate representations. Access to physical dilution refrigerators, RF wiring diagrams, and superconducting qubit fabrication blueprints is strictly prohibited.

RULE 5 [QDL-05]: Cryogenic System Emergency Venting & Safety Escalation
Category: Escalation & Facility Safety
Policy Statement:
  In the event of an unplanned vacuum breach, rapid helium pressure buildup, or cryogenic quench, automated exhaust dampers will engage. Personnel must immediately evacuate through the primary bay pressure doors. The Chief Facility Engineer and Emergency Services must be notified within 3 minutes.

RULE 6 [QDL-06]: High-Qubit Hardware Export Controls Compliance
Category: Compliance & Regulations
Policy Statement:
  Quantum processors exceeding 50 logical qubits with average gate error rates below 0.1% are subject to strategic export control dual-use regulations. All remote cloud access contracts must be screened against denied national entities, with physical compute nodes residing exclusively in sovereign domestic data centers.
