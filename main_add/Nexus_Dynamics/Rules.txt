EXECUTIVE MANDATE & COMPLIANCE OVERVIEW:
This operational directive defines the mandatory policies, code of conduct,
asset protection protocols, and escalation hierarchies governing all personnel,
systems, contractors, and advertising materials associated with this entity.
All advertising, branding, and promotional assets submitted to Inkloom AI
must comply strictly with the provisions below.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: CORE GOVERNANCE & OPERATIONAL POLICIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE 1 [NXD-01]: Access Control & Compartmented Telemetry Protocols
Category: Data Security & Access
Policy Statement:
  All flight avionics source code, CAD blueprints, and sensor telemetry must be stored in hardware-encrypted, air-gapped repositories. Personnel must authenticate via FIDO2 WebAuthn hardware keys paired with biometrics. Remote SSH access is strictly forbidden; all firmware modifications require physical console presence in an accredited SCIF (Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility).

RULE 2 [NXD-02]: Autonomous Hardware Testing & Live Range SOP
Category: Operational Conduct & SOP
Policy Statement:
  Propulsion and autonomous maneuvering field tests require a pre-flight clearance checklist signed by the Chief Test Pilot and Range Safety Officer. Live ranges must maintain redundant geo-fencing fail-safes capable of sub-second physical emergency motor cutoffs upon anomalous trajectory deviation exceeding 2.5 degrees.

RULE 3 [NXD-03]: ITAR & Defense Asset Anti-Leak Safeguards
Category: Anti-Leak & Asset Protection
Policy Statement:
  All technical documentation, schematics, and simulation outputs are subject to International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) and Export Administration Regulations (EAR). Documents must bear steganographic invisible watermarks tied to the requesting engineer's employee ID. Unauthorized export or transmission over unclassified networks constitutes a federal violation and triggers immediate termination.

RULE 4 [NXD-04]: Subcontractor & Foreign National Clean-Room Rules
Category: Compliance & Vendor Management
Policy Statement:
  External contractors and non-cleared personnel entering R&D facilities must surrender all RF-emitting personal devices at security checkpoints. Visitors must be escorted 100% of the time by a cleared security officer. Digital terminals in visitor areas operate in ephemeral, read-only clean-room sessions that purge upon session termination.

RULE 5 [NXD-05]: Critical Cyber-Avionics Escalation Hierarchy
Category: Escalation & Incident Response
Policy Statement:
  Any detected unauthorized bus injection, spoofed GPS telemetry, or zero-day vulnerability in unmanned systems must be escalated to the Defense Security Service (DSS) Liaison and CISO within 15 minutes of anomaly confirmation. The incident containment team must isolate compromised hardware segments immediately without powering down volatile memory.

RULE 6 [NXD-06]: Hardware Decommissioning & Memory Sanitization
Category: Asset Lifecycle & Disposal
Policy Statement:
  Decommissioned flight test modules, solid-state drives, and navigational guidance units must undergo a seven-pass DoD 5220.22-M sanitization process followed by physical destruction via certified industrial shredding. Destruction certificates signed by two security officers must be archived for 15 years.
