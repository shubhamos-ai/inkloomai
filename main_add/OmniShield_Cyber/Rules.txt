EXECUTIVE MANDATE & COMPLIANCE OVERVIEW:
This operational directive defines the mandatory policies, code of conduct,
asset protection protocols, and escalation hierarchies governing all personnel,
systems, contractors, and advertising materials associated with this entity.
All advertising, branding, and promotional assets submitted to Inkloom AI
must comply strictly with the provisions below.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: CORE GOVERNANCE & OPERATIONAL POLICIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE 1 [OSC-01]: Zero-Trust Architecture & Ephemeral Bastion Access
Category: Data Security & Access
Policy Statement:
  Access to client Security Operations Center (SOC) enclaves, SIEM pipelines, and EDR consoles requires dynamic zero-trust identity verification. Access tokens expire after 45 minutes; engineers must re-authenticate with physical FIDO2 tokens. Persistent elevated root/admin accounts are prohibited across all infrastructure.

RULE 2 [OSC-02]: Multi-Tenant EDR Telemetry Cryptographic Partitioning
Category: Data Security & Access
Policy Statement:
  Endpoint detection and response (EDR) event logs, memory dumps, and packet captures from enterprise clients must be cryptographically isolated using tenant-unique master keys. Cross-tenant queries are blocked by kernel-level security filters; data at rest is re-encrypted every 90 days.

RULE 3 [OSC-03]: Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure & Zero-Day Embargo
Category: Compliance & Ethics
Policy Statement:
  Zero-day vulnerabilities discovered during offensive security assessments or red-team engagements must be sequestered in an air-gapped safe. Under no circumstances may exploits be weaponized or discussed outside the Coordinated Disclosure Committee. Affected software vendors must receive private disclosure with a 90-day remediation window.

RULE 4 [OSC-04]: Malware Analysis Sandboxing & Detonation Containment
Category: Operational Conduct & SOP
Policy Statement:
  Live malware samples, ransomware artifacts, and advanced persistent threat (APT) payloads must only be detonated in non-routable, hypervisor-isolated forensic enclaves. Hardware detonation stations must have physical ethernet disconnect switches and must be fully reimaged from golden ISO images after each analysis session.

RULE 5 [OSC-05]: Critical Severity Incident (P1) Escalation Service Level Agreement
Category: Escalation & Incident Response
Policy Statement:
  Confirmation of an active domain controller breach, exfiltration in progress, or enterprise ransomware execution across client infrastructure triggers a P1 emergency state. The Incident Response Lead must bridge directly with the client CISO and initiate emergency containment playbooks within 15 minutes of detection.

RULE 6 [OSC-06]: Operational Security (OPSEC) & Dark-Web Sourcing Guidelines
Category: Anti-Leak & Asset Protection
Policy Statement:
  Threat intelligence analysts conducting dark-web reconnaissance or threat actor tracking must utilize multi-hop virtual routing, isolated virtual machines, and vetted personas. Analysts must never use corporate assets, reveal client infrastructure indicators, or engage in illicit peer-to-peer crypto transactions without General Counsel authorization.
